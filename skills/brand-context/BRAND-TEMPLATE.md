## Logo

Reason for a mark (symbol or wordmark, and why), concept, type of mark, files in `assets/logo/`, clear space, minimum size, misuse rules.

## Typography

Families with source and licence, roles (display, heading, body, caption, UI, numerals), scale ratio, line heights per script, fallback stack.

## Color

Roles with hex and scale, proportions, contrast table (every text/background pair with its ratio), dark mode, print approximations.

## Graphic language

Imagery, icons, shapes, patterns, motion: three motifs at most, each as a rule.

## Layout

Grid, margins, spacing scale, composition rules per top channel.

## Voice

Formality and register, two sample lines in the brand's language, words used and avoided, naming conventions.

## Accessibility floor

Contrast minimums, smallest text size, never color alone.

## Guardrails

What this brand never does, as positive rules where possible.
